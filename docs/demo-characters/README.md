## Klara

Klara is the user.

- 36 years old, physiotherapist, part-time, lives in Hamburg-Eimsbüttel.
- Single parent of Ada since a separation last year. She shares care with Tobi.
- She tracks her mood to learn what keeps her steady: swimming, Ada, walks with Chuky, Jen.
- Her story is her first year as she rebuilds her routine.
  - Good weeks have swimming and play.
  - Hard days cluster around handovers and the health of Oma Hilde.
