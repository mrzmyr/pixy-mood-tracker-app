Stop the preview app and reset its data.

Usage: bun app close (--platform=<ios|android> | --target=<target>)

Options:
  --platform=<ios|android>  Simulator (ios) or emulator (android) of this checkout.
                            CLI creates and boots it.
  --target=<target>         One simlock phone. Copy the value from `bun devices list`.

Behavior:
  Simulator, emulator  Reset data, shut down device, stop this checkout's Metro,
                       prune old builds.
  Phone                Stop app, release the simlock lease, prune old builds.
                       simlock removes apps installed during the lease.

Examples:
  bun app close --platform=android
  bun app close --target=pixel-8-09yw

Errors:
  missing_option              Neither --platform nor --target passed
  conflicting_options         Both --platform and --target passed
  invalid_platform            --platform is not ios or android
  target_not_found            No simlock phone has this target
  simlock_not_configured      SIMLOCK_URL or SIMLOCK_TOKEN not set
  simlock_unreachable         simlock did not answer
  phone_blocked               Phone not reachable from its simlock worker
  device_in_use               Another run holds this device
  app_clear_failed            App data was not removed
  simulator_shutdown_timeout  Simulator did not shut down in 30 seconds
  simulator_erase_failed      Simulator was not erased

Exit codes: 0 ok, 1 failure, 2 usage error
